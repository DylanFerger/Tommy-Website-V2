Working with Fitz Art

Simple, transparent, and collaborative process to bring your mural to life.

----------------------------------------

Initial Consultation

Video meeting to discuss mural location, vision, and project goals

Proposal & Deposit

Recieve a tailored proposal based on our discussion

Secure the project with a signed contract and a 50% deposit

Design Discovery

Creation of a mood board to explore styles, colors, and themes

Ensure design direction aligns with your preferences and vision

Concept Development

*First Draft* Presentation of initial design concept

*Revision Round 1* Incorporate feedback to refine the design

*Second Draft* Presentaion of adjusted design

*Final Revisions* Make small tweaks before approval

*Revisions are limited to 2 rounds for efficiency*

Final Design Approval

Approve the finished design, finalizing all details before production

Mural Installation

Installation of the mural on-site, bringing the approved design to life

Client Walkthrough

Review the completed mural together to ensure it meets your expectations

Final Payment

Make the final payment upon approval to close out the project
